Farm Master
Digital ecosystem — prototype
Clickable demo

One farm-gate to market platform for Ghana's growers, buyers, and input vendors.

Farm Master connects three sides of the agricultural value chain in a single ecosystem — matching buyer demand to farmer production, and farmer needs to trusted vendors, all coordinated through regional fulfilment centres.

1

Buyer submits demand. Crop, grade, quantity, and delivery window, with a commitment fee.

2

Farm Master matches & guides. Agronomists issue a production formula to registered farmers.

3

Vendors supply inputs. Seeds, fertiliser and machine rentals delivered to the farm gate.

4

Logistics moves the harvest. Fulfilment centres aggregate crops and trunk them to Tema.
